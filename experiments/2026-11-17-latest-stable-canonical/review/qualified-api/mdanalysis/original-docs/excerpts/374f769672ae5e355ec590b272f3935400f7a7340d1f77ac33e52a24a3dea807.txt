solution

Results are saved in this dictionary under the following keys:

results: The raw results of the time autocorrelation function.

time: Time axis, in ps, for the results.

fit: Results of the exponential curve fitting procedure. For the continuous lifetime these are (A1, tau1, tau2), for the intermittent lifetime these are (A1, A2, tau1, tau2, tau3).

tau: Calculated time constant from the fit.

estimate: Estimated values generated by the calculated fit.

The results and time values are only filled after the run() method has completed, fit, tau and estimate are filled after the solve() method has been used.

Type:

dict
