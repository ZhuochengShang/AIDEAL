Instantiates a filter which adds a text caption to an image.
The opacity of the caption will be 100% opaque.
Parameters:
caption
- The text of the caption.
font
- The font of the caption.
c
- The color of the caption.
position
- The position of the caption.
insets
- The inset size around the caption. Cannot be negative.
